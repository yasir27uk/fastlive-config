FastLive Privacy Policy

Applies to the FastLive app for Amazon Fire TV and Android TV. Last updated 20 November 2026.

FastLive has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers or what you watch.

Data kept only on your TV

These stay on your device. They are not sent to us, and they are excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfer:

  • Your favourites, recently watched channels and settings (region, playback options, custom playlist and guide addresses).
  • Your parental PIN, stored only as a one-way salted hash, never as the digits themselves.
  • A cached channel list and programme guide.

Uninstalling FastLive or clearing its data in your TV's settings deletes all of it.

Anonymous diagnostics

To keep channels working, FastLive can send a small diagnostic report after it refreshes its channel list. This only happens when FastLive's service has diagnostics enabled, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → About & legal → Share anonymous diagnostics. A report contains:

SentWhy
A random install number created by the app (not linked to your device, account or identity)To count how many installs are affected by a problem
App version and channel-list versionTo spot a bad update
Whether the channel refresh worked, and for each channel source: its short id, whether it loaded, how many channels it had, and a fixed error label such as dns or http_404To find and replace broken sources
How many times playback was started and how many attempts failed since the last reportTo measure reliability

Reports never contain channel names, programmes, stream addresses, viewing history, or your IP address in stored form. Switching diagnostics off deletes the install number and any counts not yet sent. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Other services your TV contacts

FastLive does not host video. When you watch a channel, your TV connects directly to that broadcaster's streaming servers, and channel logos and listings are downloaded from their publishers (such as the public iptv-org directory on GitHub). Those services receive your IP address and the request, as with any website, under their own privacy policies.

Children

FastLive does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Adult channels are excluded, and parents can lock further categories with a PIN.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if what FastLive collects changes. Questions or requests: open an issue.
